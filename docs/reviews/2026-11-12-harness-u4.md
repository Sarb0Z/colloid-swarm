## Rulings on the reviewer's ambiguities

By the lead, open to reversal:

- A record covers a file only when its range touched the file (finding 1).
  The plan's sentence, "its content at HEAD equals its content at some
  record's tip", read alone admits a record that never saw the change.
- A round records one `Covers:` line per lane (finding 9).
