## Review of the review

A fourth cell (opus), given the artifact and these tables but not the
reviewers' reasoning, reran C1 to C4 and C6 and labelled all nineteen
findings supported. It judged C5's severity a P2: the refusal fails closed and
its harm is the wording C14 already carries. It named three gaps the P1 fixes
need:

- G1. The rename hole of C4 is older than this unit and open in worktree
  lanes too: `wg.changed_files` and `wg.committed_files` list only a rename's
  destination (reproduced on a worktree lane).
- G2. The unbound accept of C3 is older too: a worktree lane's accept records
  the live branch tip, and its reviewer brief names the branch, not a commit.
- G3. C1's fix must reach `check --recorded` and `teardown`: a main lane's
  completion is judged from state alone, so a lane-path commit made after a
  passing `check` still passes the session end.

## Operator rulings (2026-10-16)

- Ruling 33 (C1, C2): `check` tests a one-writer run in a short-lived worktree
  at the lane's accepted commit, provisioned, and removed after a pass; the
  lane itself still works in the main tree.
- Ruling 34 (C5): a commit in a main lane's range that touches its paths and
  others is listed as mixed for the reviewer and never blocks `submit`.
