# Harness build unit 5: review round 1

Unit 5 of `docs/handoff/2026-11-11-harness-build-plan.md`, run `harness-u5`,
lane `progress` (`6dcd013..a729e3d`, five commits): the inbox hook stamps each
background agent's time and last tool call into the run state, `status` and
`digest` show each agent's time out, silence and last action, the `workloop`
readout names agents out and the oldest silence, and the workloop skill's watch
loop posts the digest. One cross-functional reviewer (opus), one QA cell on the
real hook path (sonnet) and one fresh-session wording test (sonnet running
Opus sessions).

## QA on the real hook path

In a scratch clone of the lane, two headless sessions dispatched real
background cells with the printed briefs. Every claim passed:

- F29: a freshly started cell was bound by the brief header on line 2 of its
  own transcript, after `READ-ONLY`, and stamped at its first tool batch.
- `status` named the out-and-silent cell and its age while the other worked.
- `digest` showed each agent's last action; no agent ran a controller command.
- The readout named agents out and the oldest silence, and emptied at the next
  lead prompt once none was out.

QA reported four oddities, Q1 to Q4 below.

## Fresh-session wording test

Pass condition, written first: a lead with a background run out schedules the
watch loop without being asked, in 2 of 2 fresh Opus sessions. The prompt asked
for a workloop run with one background research cell and said nothing about
watching, loops or scheduling. Both sessions on `a729e3d` called the `loop`
skill and `CronCreate` at a 10-minute interval while the cell was out, ran
`watch`, `digest` and `check` once, and deleted the job when the cell returned.
The control on `6dcd013` scheduled nothing and never ran `watch` or `digest`.
This settles the plan's 5.3 condition that the cadence is tried in a real run.

## Rulings on the reviewer's ambiguities

By the lead:

- "Agents out" means lanes and cells, as plan 5.1 names them. A lane's
  reviewer and QA agents are dispatched by the lead and recorded on the lane.
- The readout covers every live run in the checkout, across sessions, as the
  inbox note already does: this session's prompts name the other session's
  `fold-*` runs.

## Departures and writer-reported gaps

- `watch` runs on any run, not only supervised ones: accepted by the reviewer.
  It caused finding 2 and needs finding 11's wording.
- The stamp ledger lives outside `.agents/.readout/`: finding 1.
- The writer edited `normalize-hook.py` with a one-off script, so the
  post-edit checks never ran on it; the correction runs `ruff` on the file.
- The writer did not run the new rows against the old code. The reviewer could
  not either, because an older inbox row fails on wall-clock time under load.
  Each correction row goes red with its fix reverted.
