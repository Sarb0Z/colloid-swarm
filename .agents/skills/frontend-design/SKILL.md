This skill produces distinctive, production-grade frontend interfaces that escape the generic "AI slop" aesthetic. The output is real working code, every aesthetic detail and creative choice deliberate.

The user supplies the requirements — a component, page, application, or interface — and sometimes the purpose, the audience, or the technical constraints.

## Design thinking

Before the first line of code, read the context and commit to a bold aesthetic direction along four questions:
- **Purpose.** What problem the interface solves, and for whom.
- **Tone.** An extreme, chosen: brutally minimal, maximalist chaos, retro-futuristic, organic and natural, luxury and refined, playful and toy-like, editorial and magazine, brutalist and raw, art deco and geometric, soft and pastel, industrial and utilitarian, among the many others the space holds. These are prompts, not a menu; the design is one true to its own direction.
- **Constraints.** The technical requirements: framework, performance, accessibility.
- **Differentiation.** What makes it unforgettable — the one detail someone carries away.

A clear conceptual direction, executed with precision, is the method. Bold maximalism and refined minimalism both succeed; what decides is intentionality, not intensity.

The implementation — HTML, CSS and JS, React, Vue, or any other stack — is then production-grade and functional, visually striking and memorable, cohesive under one aesthetic point of view, and refined in every detail.

## Frontend aesthetics

- **Typography.** Fonts that are beautiful, unique, and interesting. Generic faces such as Arial and Inter give way to distinctive, characterful, unexpected choices that lift the whole surface. Pair a distinctive display face with a refined body face.
- **Color and theme.** One cohesive aesthetic, held in CSS variables for consistency. Dominant colors with sharp accents outperform timid, evenly distributed palettes.
- **Motion.** Animation for effects and micro-interactions: CSS-only for plain HTML, the Motion library for React when it is available. Spend it on high-impact moments — one well-orchestrated page load with staggered reveals (`animation-delay`) delights more than scattered micro-interactions — and on scroll-triggered and hover states that surprise.
- **Spatial composition.** Unexpected layouts: asymmetry, overlap, diagonal flow, grid-breaking elements, and either generous negative space or controlled density.
- **Backgrounds and visual detail.** Atmosphere and depth in place of a default solid color: contextual effects and textures matched to the aesthetic — gradient meshes, noise textures, geometric patterns, layered transparencies, dramatic shadows, decorative borders, custom cursors, grain overlays.

The generic-AI-aesthetic set is off the palette: overused font families (Inter, Roboto, Arial, system fonts), cliched color schemes (purple gradients on white above all), predictable layouts and component patterns, and cookie-cutter design without context-specific character.

Interpret creatively and make unexpected choices that read as designed for this context. No two designs are alike: vary light and dark themes, fonts, and aesthetics, choosing afresh each generation rather than converging on a house favorite (Space Grotesk, for example).

Implementation complexity matches the vision. Maximalist designs need elaborate code with extensive animation and effects; minimalist or refined designs need restraint, precision, and close attention to spacing, typography, and subtle detail. Elegance is the vision executed well, and the vision, once chosen, is executed in full, without hedging.
